## 2.2.0 (2026-10-14) - closed in its current form

- Selection-protocol results: v1.0 and v1.1 (k = 12 and k = 6) and v1.2.1 (SG x S^1 with spin structure, 10 configurations, k = 8) all gave *no evidence*; v1.1 and v1.2.1 could have reached the threshold. Ledger: 193 recorded configuration evaluations, plus the earlier carpet search (about 128), so at least about 321.
- Ledger corrected: carpet search recorded; cumulative counts stated as lower bounds. Exploratory cluster-structure check (row 6).
- Pre-registration documents: v1.2 draft (design only) and v1.2.1 (family, rule, null-only choice of k).
- Open Problem #2: exact counts up to level 10; the spread of regression-based exponent fits is a property of the estimator (phase of a 3-period window), not of the level; a window-free chord estimator recovers ln3/ln5 to better than 0.1% (level 9, N >= 1000). Decimation holds only up to corner-defect corrections for this Laplacian.
- README: final status, corrected and added reproduction instructions, open problems updated.
