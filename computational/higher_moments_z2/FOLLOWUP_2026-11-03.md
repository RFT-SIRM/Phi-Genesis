# H10 follow-up: finite structural explanation

**Date:** 3 November 2026  
**Status:** exact finite calculations and structural checks only. No all-level theorem or Lean 4 formalization is claimed.

## 1. Full-flux defect

For the deterministic GF(2) representative (non-pivot edge variables set to zero), define

\[
L_m=D_m-A_{s,m},\qquad
D_m^{(10)}=\mathrm{Tr}(L_{\pi,m}^{10})-\mathrm{Tr}(L_{0,m}^{10}).
\]

The exact sparse-integer calculation uses \(\mathrm{Tr}(L^{10})=\|L^5\|_F^2\). Direct full-flux traces were computed through level 10. The residual

\[
D_m^{(10)}-3D_{m-1}^{(10)}
\]

is 12,387,360 on transitions 3→4 through 9→10. The earlier transitions are exceptional. These values are in full_flux_h10_levels_1_10.csv.

## 2. Generation-mask recurrence

Every generation mask was directly evaluated at levels 3–9: 1,016 exact traces. All values are distinct within each level. All 1,008 parent/new-bit transition checks for parent levels 3–8 match

\[
F_{m+1}=3F_m+Q(t_1,t_2,t_3,t_{m+1}).
\]

With \(a=t_1,b=t_2,c=t_3,d=t_{m+1}\),

\[
\begin{aligned}
Q(a,b,c,d)={}&127\,182\,024+89\,195\,520a+73\,945\,440b-145\,153\,920ab\\
&+55\,360c-150\,880ac+40\,320bc-39\,680abc\\
&+d(115\,347\,760-110\,022\,480a+10\,024\,960b\\
&\qquad-20\,854\,720ab+320c-640ac).
\end{aligned}
\]

Here \(Q(0,0,0,0)=127\,182\,024\) and \(Q(1,1,1,1)=139\,569\,384\); their difference is exactly 12,387,360. The 16 values are recorded in Q_truth_table_16.csv. This is still a finite-data recurrence, not an all-level theorem.

## 3. Three-copy explanation

Write the recurrence correction as \(Q=P+G\), where \(P\) is the sum of changes in the three restricted child traces and \(G\) is the parent trace minus the sum of the child traces.

For all 112 masks at parent levels 4–6, the local child corrections are

\[
\begin{aligned}
P_0={}&d(78\,396\,480-110\,751\,600a+4\,704\,800b\\
&\qquad-10\,553\,280ab+160c-320ac),\\
P_1={}&0,\\
P_2={}&a(-32\,355\,120-5\,848\,480b-160c).
\end{aligned}
\]

The pairwise gluing terms factor through diagonal moments at the shared corner. For copies \(i,j\), with \(u_{i,ij}(k)=(L_i^k)_{x_{ij},x_{ij}}\),

\[
G_{ij}=\sum_{r=1}^{5}\frac{10}{r}
\sum_{\substack{\sum_q(\alpha_q+\beta_q)=10\\\alpha_q,\beta_q\ge1}}
\prod_{q=1}^{r}u_{i,ij}(\alpha_q)u_{j,ij}(\beta_q).
\]

On all 112 masks, \(G=G_{01}+G_{02}+G_{12}\) exactly. The 336 corner-moment observations reduce to 48 signatures, each stable across parent levels 4–6. This gives a concrete finite mechanism for Q; uniform stability at every level remains open.

## 4. Invariance boundary

For vertex switching \(S=\mathrm{diag}(\epsilon_v)\), \(\epsilon_v\in\{-1,+1\}\), the signed Laplacian changes to \(SLS\), so \(\mathrm{Tr}((SLS)^{10})=\mathrm{Tr}(L^{10})\). The genuine switching invariant belongs to the full signed-graph switching class.

Hole parities alone do not fix that class. At level 5, the cycle-space rank is 364 and the hole-boundary constraint rank is 121, leaving 243 independent cycle directions. All 243 individually tested boundary-invisible edge flips preserve the prescribed hole parities and change H10. They produce 48 distinct trace changes. Therefore 12,387,360 is not an invariant of hole flux alone.

A fixed boundary-invisible edge flip changes the exact trace by 111,750,160 in direct whole-matrix checks at levels 4–7. The same radius-9 signed local operator patch (52 vertices, 107 incident edges) is identical through levels 4–9. Repeating that local perturbation changes the residual to

\[
12\,387\,360-2(111\,750\,160)=-211\,112\,960.
\]

## 5. Conditional consequence and next proof

If the observed full-flux recurrence held for every \(m\ge3\), then

\[
D_m^{(10)}=555\,046\,440\cdot3^{m-3}-6\,193\,680
\]

and, since \(|V_m|=(3^{m+1}+3)/2\),

\[
\lim_{m\to\infty}\frac{D_m^{(10)}}{|V_m|}
=\frac{370\,030\,960}{27}.
\]

This limit is conditional, not a proved invariant. The next proof target is uniform stability of the two child-restriction rules and the nine corner moments, followed by the three-copy induction and Lean 4 formalization. No complete level-11 trace was obtained.
