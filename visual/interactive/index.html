02 / Mathematical anchor

The known exponent is a reference point

Separate established mathematical structure from finite-level estimation and physical interpretation.

Sierpiński gasket · theoretical spectral exponent
\[\frac{d_s}{2}=\frac{\ln 3}{\ln 5}\approx0.6826\]

The factor-5 decimation structure produces log-periodic signatures. Numerical estimates at finite graph levels depend on the chosen estimator and counting window; agreement with the known exponent is a consistency check, not a new prediction.

The proposed mass relation \(m=A n\) uses a fitted scale and target indices \(\{1,12,17,40\}\). The indices have not been independently derived from a spectral invariant. The relation is a fit, not a prediction.
