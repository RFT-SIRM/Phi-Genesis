02 / Principal mathematical result

The limiting spectral constant

The infinite-level limit is defined by a proved convergence theorem and enclosed by exact rational endpoints.

Lean 4 · exact rational enclosure
\[\lambda_\infty\in[L,U],\qquad U-L=2\cdot10^{-36}\]
\[L=\frac{1309128920280813421888623088170810187}{10^{36}},\quad U=\frac{1309128920280813421888623088170810189}{10^{36}}\]

For every level N ≥ 6, the selected Schur root is the least eigenvalue. These least eigenvalues converge to the number in the interval above.

The level-9 value is computed numerically to about 145 displayed digits. Those digits belong to the finite graph at level 9; they are not a 145-digit proof for the infinite-level limit.

03 / New finite computation · November 2026

Exact tenth moments of the signed Z₂ Laplacian

A separate scalar-operator calculation, with exact integer traces and an explicit boundary on what has—and has not—been proved.

Deterministic hole-flux representative · levels 1–10
\[D_m^{(10)}=\operatorname{Tr}(L_{\pi,m}^{10})-\operatorname{Tr}(L_{0,m}^{10})\]
\[D_m^{(10)}-3D_{m-1}^{(10)}=12,387,360\quad (3\to4,\ldots,9\to10)\]

Exact sparse-integer calculations reproduce the tenth-moment difference through level 10. The first two transitions are exceptional; the constant residual is observed from 3→4 onward in this finite range.

Finite computational result—not an all-level theorem. The GF(2) solver chooses a deterministic representative by setting non-pivot edge variables to zero. Hole-boundary parities alone do not determine the cocycle: at level 5, 243 elementary-triangle holonomy degrees of freedom remain, and changing the local representative changes H10.

Levels 4–9

156 direct samples
