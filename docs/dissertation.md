## Recursive Graph Operators, Spectral Trace Defects, and Higher-Moment Recurrence

**Author:** Evgeny Zaprudnov  
**Research group:** RFT-SIRM  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 15 October 2026

The dissertation consolidates research on trace defects of recursively constructed graph operators, Sierpiński-gasket approximations, SU(2)-connected operators, higher even trace moments, and a separate programme on signed Z2-flux Laplacians and spectral decimation. It distinguishes formalized results, exact finite-level certificates, finite numerical checks, and conjectures.

This documentation page is a short orientation only. To preserve mathematical notation and formula formatting exactly, the complete dissertation remains available in its original Markdown source.
