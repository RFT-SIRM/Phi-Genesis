# Research Strategy

![RFT-SIRM](https://img.shields.io/badge/RFT--SIRM-Research-174ea6?style=flat-square) ![Phi-Genesis](https://img.shields.io/badge/Phi--Genesis-Spectral%20Research-147fba?style=flat-square) ![Evidence standard](https://img.shields.io/badge/standard-evidence--first-168f83?style=flat-square)


The next research phase should prioritize closure conditions and reproducibility over expanding the number of exploratory searches.

## Priorities

1. Resolve the window dependence of finite-level spectral-exponent estimates with a declared estimator and uncertainty analysis.
2. Specify a mathematically independent candidate rule for the target indices before evaluating new data.
3. Design null-only power checks that can reach the declared decision threshold.
4. Record every tested family, exclusions, parameter choices, and stopping rule.
5. Preserve negative outcomes and distinguish numerical evidence from theorem-level conclusions.

## Release discipline

No new selection claim should be promoted unless the rule is independently specified, its test is adequately powered, and the result survives the declared multiple-testing and dependence controls.

See [Open Problems](open-problems.md), [Methodology](methodology.md), and [Selection Protocol](selection-protocol.md).
