![RFT-SIRM](https://img.shields.io/badge/RFT--SIRM-Research-174ea6?style=flat-square) ![Phi-Genesis](https://img.shields.io/badge/Phi--Genesis-Spectral%20Research-147fba?style=flat-square) ![Evidence standard](https://img.shields.io/badge/standard-evidence--first-168f83?style=flat-square)


## Purpose

This page records the role of finite-level experiments in the Phi-Genesis research programme. They are computational checks on specified graph families and parameter choices, not physical-device experiments.

## Evidence summary

- Spectral-decimation and gap patterns are reproduced numerically within the documented scope.
- Finite-level spectral-exponent estimates vary with the counting window and estimator.
- Preregistered target-selection tests did not meet the required threshold.
- The claimed \(\eta\)-invariant value \(-4\) was not reproduced in the documented tests.

## Reporting requirements

Every reported trial should identify the graph level, number of vertices, operator and boundary convention, algorithm, precision, parameters, and any failed or excluded cases.

See [Verified Results](verified-results.md), [Selection Protocol](selection-protocol.md), and [Reproducibility](reproducibility.md).
