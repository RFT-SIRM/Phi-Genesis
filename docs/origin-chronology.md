This page records the documented research sequence without inventing dates or implying that later work validates earlier hypotheses.

## Research trail

1. **Spectral observations on gasket-like graph families.** Initial numerical patterns motivated questions about decimation, gaps, and target indices.
2. **Broader family searches.** Additional operator and configuration families were examined; the growing search space made the look-elsewhere effect central to interpretation.
3. **Preregistered tests.** The selection hypothesis was tested with declared thresholds and null models. The documented tests did not meet the required threshold.
4. **Methodology correction.** Underpowered designs and post-selection risks were explicitly recorded rather than treated as positive evidence.
5. **Current status — v2.2.1.** The project reports bounded numerical observations, a still-open finite-window exponent question, and no independently established target-index rule.
