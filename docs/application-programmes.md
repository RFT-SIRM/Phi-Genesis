# Phi-Genesis Research Programmes

**Purpose:** a source-linked index of the distinct research programmes within the spectral audit. The sections identify research scope and evidence boundaries; they do not certify a physical interpretation.

## Spectral decimation and gap structure

The project examines the factor-five spectral-decimation structure and Fukushima–Shima gaps on finite Sierpiński-gasket graphs. Results must be interpreted with the documented corner-defect correction for the corner-degree-2 graph Laplacian.

See [Verified Results](verified-results.md) and [Computational Architecture](computational-architecture.md).

## Boundary conditions and flux

Möbius and \(\pi\)-flux configurations are used to test how boundary conditions affect spectral gaps. A gap in a tested configuration is not evidence that the target indices \(\{1,12,17,40\}\) have been independently selected.

See [Verified Results](verified-results.md).

## Spectral counting and exponent estimation

The theoretical reference is \(d_s/2=\ln 3/\ln 5\). Finite-level estimates show window-dependent spread; Open Problem #2 remains open.

See [Open Problems](open-problems.md).
