# Phi-Genesis Research Programmes

The principal mathematical result is the convergence theorem for the least eigenvalues of the recursively defined Sierpiński-gasket graph operators.

## Schur recursion and limiting spectral constant

An exact three-corner boundary Schur recursion is connected to the graph matrices in Lean 4. Its selected root is the least eigenvalue for every level $N\geq6$, and the sequence converges to $\lambda_\infty$ inside an exact rational interval of width $2\cdot10^{-36}$.

See the [spectral-limit dossier](spectral-limit.md), [Lean proof map](https://github.com/RFT-SIRM/Phi-Genesis/blob/main/computational/spectral_gap_theta_2pi/PROOF_MAP.md), and [formal source files](https://github.com/RFT-SIRM/Phi-Genesis/tree/main/PhiGenesis/SpectralGap).

## Supporting mathematical and computational strands

- Signed local-face operator and gauge equivalence.
- Spectral decimation and finite-level gap structure.
- Boundary conditions and flux calculations.
- Spectral dimension and finite-level counting.
- Exact finite signed-$Z_2$ H10 moment calculations.

Each strand retains its own proof or computation boundary and does not replace the main Schur-limit theorem.
