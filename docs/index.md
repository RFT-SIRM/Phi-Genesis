# Phi-Genesis — Limiting Spectral Constant

Phi-Genesis studies spectral operators on recursively refined Sierpiński-gasket graphs. Its principal result is a Lean 4 proof that the least-eigenvalue sequence selected by an exact Schur boundary recursion converges to a limit in an exact rational interval of width $2\cdot10^{-36}$.

The detailed theorem and its boundaries are documented in the [Schur recursion and limiting spectral constant dossier](spectral-limit.md).
