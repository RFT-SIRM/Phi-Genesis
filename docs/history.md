# Historical Development and Provenance

This page links to the chronological record of Phi-Genesis. Earlier exploratory questions and their later reassessment remain part of the historical record; they are not the project's principal mathematical result.

## Principal mathematical development

1. Graph and operator definitions for the recursively refined Sierpiński gasket.
2. Derivation of the exact three-corner Schur boundary recursion.
3. Lean proof connecting the scalar recursion to the graph-matrix boundary response.
4. Identification of the selected root with the least eigenvalue for every level $N\geq6$.
5. Proof of convergence to $\lambda_\infty$ inside the exact rational interval.
6. Current frontier: a sharper certified enclosure, the quadratic increment law, and unconditional external-operator identification.
