## Evidence-first chronology

1. **Spectral observations:** reproduce finite-level spectral-decimation and gap calculations, including corner-defect corrections.
2. **Candidate mass indices:** investigate whether the proposed indices \(\{1,12,17,40\}\) are selected independently of the mass data.
3. **Preregistered tests:** evaluate fixed rules against their declared null ensembles and thresholds.
4. **Negative findings:** preserve failed selection attempts, power limitations, and look-elsewhere corrections in the record.
5. **Current status:** no independent selection rule has been established; Open Problem #2 remains open because finite-window exponent estimates depend on estimator and window choice.
