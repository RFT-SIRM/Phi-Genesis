# Interactive Research Portal

Open the [animated Phi-Genesis spectral research portal](https://rft-sirm.github.io/Phi-Genesis/interactive/).

The portal is a separate, animated research navigation surface for the Sierpiński-gasket spectral audit, boundary-condition experiments, selection-protocol results, and open problems. It is a navigational visualization; the source repository, versioned protocol records, and reproducibility instructions remain authoritative.

- [Research map](research-map.md)

- [Selection protocol and negative results](selection-protocol.md)
- [Research history](history.html)
- [Main Phi-Genesis repository](https://github.com/RFT-SIRM/Phi-Genesis)

The portal does not establish an independent selection rule for the target indices `{1, 12, 17, 40}`. Its status and claims must be read with the linked research records; visualizations are not a substitute for the documented tests.
