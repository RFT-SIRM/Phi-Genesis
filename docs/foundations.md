# Foundations: Graph Spectra and Evidence

## Spectral anchor

For the Sierpiński gasket, the theoretical spectral exponent is

\[
\frac{d_s}{2}=\frac{\ln 3}{\ln 5}\approx 0.6826.
\]

The factor-five scaling is a reference structure for finite-level spectral-decimation experiments. The corner-degree-2 graph Laplacian requires the documented corner-defect corrections.

## Boundary conditions

Möbius and \(\pi\)-flux variants test how boundary conditions alter finite graph spectra and gap structure. Observing a gap does not identify a unique mass index or establish a physical mechanism.

## Evidence vocabulary

The project distinguishes numerical reproduction, preregistered statistical outcomes, fitted relationships, and open conjectures. A fitted relationship \(m=A n\) is not an independently derived prediction; the target set \(\{1,12,17,40\}\) remains unselected by an established invariant.
